LearnBridge AI

Adaptive AI Learning Platform

AI Machine Learning Education React Express Tailwind CSS

An adaptive learning platform that builds a dynamic model of what a student knows, diagnoses learning gaps, and continuously adapts the learning path.

Unlike a conventional AI chatbot that simply answers questions, LearnBridge maintains a Student Knowledge Model and uses it to diagnose weaknesses, recommend what to learn next, generate targeted interventions, and reassess progress.

CHALLENGE ML Empowerment Build Challenge 2026
TYPE AI / Machine Learning / Education
FOCUS Personalized Learning
PLATFORM Online
THE PROBLEM

Most learning platforms tell students what they completed, but don't deeply understand WHY they are struggling. LearnBridge approaches learning as a continuously changing knowledge model.

Certificate of Participation ML Empowerment Build Challenge 2.0 • Sept 4, 2026

CORE CAPABILITIES

01
Dynamic Knowledge Model

Tracks mastery, confidence, retention, and concept dependencies instead of relying on a simple progress bar.

02
AI Learning Diagnosis

Identifies patterns in mistakes and evaluates conceptual understanding, problem solving, application, and retention.

03
Adaptive Learning Agent

Assess → Recommend → Intervene → Reassess. The system dynamically creates targeted learning sessions based on identified weaknesses.

04
AI Mentorship

Includes an AI Coding Mentor, Teach Back Mode, and Socratic Tutor for interactive guided learning.
